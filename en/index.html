01 / ABOUT

Tarık ASLAN

I am a lecturer at Bandırma Onyedi Eylül University. My research brings together image processing, computer vision, mechatronics, and occupational health and safety.

Previously: İstanbul Okan University (2016–2021) and Karadeniz Technical University (2021–2025). At Bandırma Onyedi Eylül University since 2025.
